About

Hi! My name is Igor and I'm currently studying and expanding my knowledge in audio and coding.

I'm finishing my studies at Digital Arts and Entertainment in Belgium with a degree in Game Sound Integration, combining the creative process of sound design with the technical side of implementation.

On one hand, the program focuses on working in DAWs, finding the right audio, editing and implementing it. On the other, the technical side: working with game engines (Unity, Unreal), middleware (FMOD, Wwise), understanding the physics of audio (reflections, occlusion, transmission, raycasting) and integrating audio using code (C#, C++) or via Blueprints.

Currently I'm also working at XR Labs, where I lead technical sound design for VR/AR training simulations - police, military, customs. People who need the audio to feel real, because they're training for situations that are.
