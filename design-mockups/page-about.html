About

Sound designer, programmer, problem solver.

I'm Igor Szuniewicz, a game audio student at Howest DAE in Belgium. I work the full audio pipeline — from recording foley and designing sounds to implementing them in Wwise, FMOD, and Unreal Engine.

I got into game audio because I wanted to make the sounds people hear when they play. Not music (though I do that too) — the footsteps, the ambient hum of a spaceship, the click of a menu button. The stuff that makes a game feel real.

Before Belgium, I studied at a bilingual high school in Poland and attended music school. I speak English, Polish, and Dutch.

How I Work

Mix Hierarchy

Every sound has a priority. I build mixes where the most important thing is always clear.

Budget Awareness

Voice counts, memory limits, CPU. I design audio that ships, not audio that demos.

Parametric Sound

One asset, many variations. RTPCs, randomization, and procedural generation over brute-force samples.

Collaboration

Audio touches everything. I work closely with designers, programmers, and artists.
