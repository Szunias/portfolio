the concept

a first-person horror game where sound is your only tool. no ui markers, no minimap, no health bar — just your ears. players navigate dark environments and detect threats through spatial audio. if you can hear it, you can survive it. if you can't, well.

audio systems i built

threat detection

distance-based audio system where enemy sounds intensify as they approach. wwise rtpcs driven by blueprint distance calculations, with hysteresis thresholds to prevent audio flickering at boundary distances. enemies sound different depending on how close they are — a subtle rumble at 20m becomes a visceral growl at 3m.

environmental storytelling

each room has a unique ambient signature. i layered 4–6 ambient stems per space — dripping pipes, electrical hum, distant screams, wind through broken walls. wwise states crossfade between them as players move. you know which room you're in before you see it.

adaptive music

the score responds to player stress. i built a tension meter that tracks proximity to threats, player health, and time in darkness. it drives music intensity through wwise switches — from dead silence to full orchestral dread. no two playthroughs sound the same.

stuff that went wrong (and how i fixed it)

// problem
32 voice limit on console meant every sound had to earn its slot. dense combat scenes with ambient layers were consistently exceeding the budget. sounds were dropping out mid-scare.
// what i did
built a priority system with distance-based culling. sounds farther than 15m get virtualized; combat sounds always take priority over ambient. added a fallback bus for critical audio that never gets cut.
// result
zero audible dropouts across 40+ hours of playtesting
// problem
players couldn't locate enemies by sound alone. stereo panning wasn't giving enough spatial information for the core "listen to survive" mechanic. people were walking straight into threats.
// what i did
switched from stereo panning to binaural hrtf rendering in wwise. tuned the spatialization curves per enemy type for distinct distance signatures. each creature has its own spatial profile.
// result
player navigation accuracy improved +40% in blind playtest sessions
