The Project

The Concept

A stone giant wakes up in a cave and discovers dance. That's the film. The original composer dropped out a week before the screening, so I got the call. Eight days to write a disco-funk score, do all the sound design, and deliver a final mix.

I pulled the animatics into Pro Tools, hand-placed sync markers, and matched every musical hit to Ray's movements frame by frame. Three rounds of feedback with the animation team, each time adjusting timing and arrangement. Standing ovation at the cinema screening.

Composition

The Music

Bass & Rhythm

Slap bass and electric drums. Chic, Earth Wind & Fire territory. Every rhythmic hit locked to Ray's dance moves, frame by frame.

Brass, Sax & Synths

Brass carries the melody, saxophones fill out the warmth. DJ scratches between sections keep it moving. Mixed in Pro Tools, mastered with Waves J37 tape saturation for that analog warmth.

Credits Version

Same theme, bigger arrangement. Gospel organs, live drums, Omnisphere synths layered on top. The team approved it on the first pass.
