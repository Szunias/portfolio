about me

hi, i'm igor

i'm a 21-year-old game audio student from poland, currently studying at howest dae in belgium. i moved here because it's one of the few places in europe where you can study game audio properly.

i do sound design, audio implementation, and sometimes i write code to make the audio pipeline less painful. i work with wwise, fmod, unreal, and whatever tool gets the job done.

before belgium, i went to a bilingual high school in kołobrzeg (that's a small town by the sea in poland) and studied piano and trumpet at music school. i speak english, polish, and dutch.

right now i'm looking for an internship (jan–jun 2026) where i can work on real game audio. i can relocate anywhere and i'm cool with remote too.
