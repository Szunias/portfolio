The Problem

The Concept

Unreal corrupts its own intermediate files more often than it should. You get linker errors, stale caches, builds that worked yesterday and fail today for no reason.

The fix is always the same: delete Binaries, Intermediate, Saved, regenerate the VS solution, recompile. That's 15 minutes of clicking through folders. I automated the whole thing.

One button clears the cache, regenerates project files, and kicks off a clean compile. It also shows you how much disk space each folder eats and cleans the DDC cache — that thing grows to gigabytes if you don't watch it. Auto-detects UE 4.x and 5.x installations.
