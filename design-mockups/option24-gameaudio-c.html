Transientica

Real-time audio classification tool that detects transient types in a live signal. Built the DSP pipeline in C++ with JUCE — FFT analysis, feature extraction, and an SVM classifier hitting 92% accuracy at 45ms latency. Designed for sound designers who need fast, reliable onset detection without leaving their DAW.

About

I'm a game audio student at Howest Digital Arts & Entertainment in Kortrijk, Belgium. I got into game audio through modding — replacing sounds in games I played, then wondering how the systems behind them worked.

Now I focus on the full pipeline: recording and designing assets, building interactive systems in middleware, implementing in-engine, and optimizing until the mix is clean and the memory budget is met. I care most about audio that serves the game, not audio that shows off.

When I'm not in a DAW or an engine, I'm probably cooking, playing bass, or breaking something in Unreal that I'll have to fix later.
