About

I am a 21-year-old game audio student at Howest Digital Arts & Entertainment in Kortrijk, Belgium. I build audio systems, design sounds, and write tools that make game audio work better.

I work across the full pipeline: recording and synthesis, middleware implementation in Wwise and FMOD, engine integration in Unreal (C++ and Blueprints), and optimization for real-time constraints.

When I am not working on game audio, I make music, break audio plugins to see how they work, and occasionally fix them.
