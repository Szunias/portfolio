The Concept

A first-person horror game where sound is your only tool for survival. There are no UI markers, no minimap, no health bar — just your ears. Players navigate dark environments and detect threats entirely through spatial audio cues.

Audio Systems I Built

Threat Detection System

Built a distance-based audio system where enemy sounds intensify as they approach. Used Wwise RTPCs driven by Blueprint distance calculations, with hysteresis thresholds to prevent audio flickering.

Environmental Storytelling

Each room has a unique ambient signature. I layered 4–6 ambient stems per space and used Wwise States to crossfade between them as players move.

Adaptive Music

The score responds to player stress level. I built a tension meter that drives music intensity through Wwise switches — from silence to full orchestral dread.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge
32 voice limit on console meant every sound had to earn its slot. Dense combat scenes with ambient layers were consistently exceeding the budget.
Solution
Built a priority system with distance-based culling. Sounds farther than 15m get virtualized; combat sounds always take priority over ambient.
Result
Zero audible dropouts across 40+ hours of playtesting
Challenge
Players couldn't locate enemies by sound. Stereo panning wasn't giving enough spatial information for the core listen-to-survive mechanic.
Solution
Switched from stereo panning to binaural HRTF rendering in Wwise. Tuned the spatialization curves per enemy type for distinct distance signatures.
Result
Player navigation accuracy improved 40% in blind playtest sessions
