Design Philosophy

How I approach audio design

Clarity First

Game audio needs to be clear. What does the player need to hear right now? Mix hierarchy, frequency separation, ducking.

Know Your Limits

Understanding limits helps: voice counts, memory, CPU. Build systems that scale without breaking budget.

Layers & Parameters

Design sounds in layers: transient for punch, body for weight, texture for character. Blend them in real-time.

Iterate with Feedback

Audio needs input from designers, programmers, and players. Document systems and iterate based on playtests.
