AI Audio Research

Thesis, 2026

A UE5 and Python study of generated game-audio latency. I measured 600 generations from game event to first audible sample and built a cache with a procedural DSP fallback.

The fastest tested generator reached 1.9 seconds at p95 against the prototype’s 150 ms interaction target.
