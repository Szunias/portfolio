About

Who's behind the faders

I sit in the overlap most teams are missing: production on one side — recording, mixing, composition — and implementation on the other: Wwise, FMOD, C++, Blueprints in Unreal and Unity.

My Game Sound Integration training at Digital Arts and Entertainment covered the full chain, from DAW to engine. At XR Labs I do technical sound design for VR/AR training simulations used in defence and public safety — practical work: if a threat is behind a wall, it has to sound filtered, distant, and correctly placed.

Before any of this: twelve years of classical piano. It still shows up in how I hear a mix.
