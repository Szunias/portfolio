Narration talking points:
	[Start]
	- Intro cutscene
		"We felt that a typical intro wasn't right to us. Because our goal was to make a fast-pace racing game. Or as we like to say a quick game you can jump into and walk away from. Just as a classic couch game should be."
		"So as you can see in our intro we showcase the important mechanics such as how to use an item and how to get back in the race after ben eliminated."
		"Then afterwards a pan-over, of our level so that players can see what they can expect."
		"Now ofcourse for the more familiar players, they are able to skip the cutscene. That way we can go straight to selecting the grandmas."
	- Character selection
		"As you saw in the trailer. You can choose out of 5 different grandmas, each player being able to pick one unique grandma for themselves. My favourite is Gwen [Blows kiss]."
	- Lobby
		"At first we had that the players would all have to get ready and once they where we threw instantly in the race. We have noticed that new players had trouble understanding their mechanics that way."
		"So we decided to instead do a lobby style readying up where players can take there time getting familiar with their mechanics."
		"Also being able to test the items there gives them a change to get to know them better as well."
		[Players start messing around in the lobby]
		"As you can see, it's also incredibly fun to just play around with the pickups in the lobby, especially spamming the creampie everywhere"
		"Alright enough messing around guys, Let's start the race."
		[Players press start]
		"3, 2, 1 GO!"
	- Mechanics as player
		"Mechanics for that player are very simple and easy to pick up."
		"Players can use the left stick to the left or right. And press the SOUTH controller button to jump."
		"For items they can pick up items, once a Player has a item within their possesion they can press the EAST controller button to use the item."
	- Items
		"While on the subject for items."
		"Each item has unique purpose."
		"Now an item we hold dear to our hearts. The CREAMPIE, which players can drop and be an obstacle."
		"We got the HANDPIN which is honestly the main leading item. You can throw it towards other players to try and stun them."
		"Then we got it's counter part the UMBRELLA which a player can use to protect themselves from the HANDPIN."
		"And if you don't like your race bland we got your one and only TABASCO sauce, Once you use this you gain a burst of speed. To spice up the race."
	- Map
		"Lets talk about the level."
		"Now the level itself had many iterations. We really aimed for a well designed level where the balancing between areas was satisfing. Does the jump here feel natural, would the player go here, and so on an on and on."
		"Everything went hand in hand. If you adjusted the camera speed the map had to change. Change the position of 1 pick up, again we have to look at the map."
		"But after all those iterations we found a sweet spot for the map that we have noticed had a huge postive impact on players and us."
		"We have in our maps 3 different area's all ment to have their own flow."
		"The first part the coffee corner where we aimed to have ease in kinda feeling. Where the player slowly speeds up in diffculty."
		"The second part the cafeteria where the goal was to introduce the players to more height varriation and plantforms to prepare them for the last part of the map"
		"The Library where the pressure is higher with lots of height varriation, portals and jump pads."
		"And near the finish we have placed more tabasco to really put pressure on players on the last strech.
	- Art aesthetic
		"For styles of our game. The enviorment has to be realstic, players in clay style. Making them readable and but yet still matching with the style."
		"For the map interactables we focused more on highlighting them via their unique ways. The fans show the air flow. The jump pad being more emissive and the jam being more saturated."
		"And then the RFX had to follow a sticker/drawing style, where pickups and item followed this paper style."
		"Making everything have logical relation to each other."
	- Dynamic map features
		"Now if you've paid attention closely to the map you see some dynamice features that interact with the players during the race."
		"So we got some Fans in the map that blow in the direction of the race which will increase you speed, Also do we have some Fans that blow up which make our player be able to fly while being in the air flow."
		"Cool thing as well that we have some spilled jam over the map. That will slow down the player instead."
		"To reach different heights or areas the player can use also the portals or jump pads to gain the upperhand."
	- Ball of Death
		"Lets talk about the big ball on the side of the screen, I mean it's hard to miss like come on it a big yarn ball coming from them."
		"Ofcourse when that big yarn ball squashes a player they are as one says ELIMINATED."
	- Revenge Mechanics
		"But not to worry, in our game death is not the end. I may want my revenge on the other person that eliminated me right or just another chance to win. Don't worry we got you!"
		"Once you get eliminated you get put back in the game as a ghost. To get back in the race you need to passthrough another player stealing their life to fill your own. And now you can have your revenge!!"
	- Race
		"Now with all of these mechanics you can race your friends to the finish line winning the price of being first. Again, and Again, and again"
	
	[End]
