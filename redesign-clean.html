About01

I design and implement gameplay audio that stays readable under real constraints — voice limits, memory budgets, dense moment-to-moment mixes.

My Game Sound Integration work at Digital Arts and Entertainment sits between production and implementation: DAWs, recording and mixing on one side; Wwise, FMOD, C++ and Blueprints on the other. At XR Labs I do technical sound design for VR/AR training simulations used in defence and public safety — if a threat is behind a wall, it has to sound filtered, distant, and correctly placed.
