About

At XR Labs Belgium, between February and June 2026, I handled sound on selected Unity VR and AR training simulations. In my own projects, I connect sound to player actions and build tools that solve recurring audio problems.

I studied Game Sound Integration at Howest DAE and trained in classical piano at the State Music School in Kołobrzeg.
