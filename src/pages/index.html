PROFILE

About

I work where sound design meets game code. At XR Labs Belgium, between February and June 2026, I handled sound on selected Unity VR and AR training simulations. In my own projects, I connect sound to player actions and build tools that solve recurring audio problems.

I am based in Kortrijk, Belgium, studied Game Sound Integration at Howest DAE and have a twelve-year background in classical piano. I am open to technical sound design and game audio implementation roles.
