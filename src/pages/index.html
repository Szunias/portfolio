Selected work

  • HUSHABYE

    A horror game played with a microphone, which I am making solo in Unity 6 with Wwise and my own C# DSP.

Tools

  • AutoTrim Exporter

    A REAPER tool I designed and built for delivering sound effects. It trims the silence around each sound and checks every render before delivery.

    2026 · Lua ReaScript, ReaImGui View the workflow →

  • AudioQ

    A C++/JUCE sampler: loop a recording, cut it into grains, crush and stutter it.

    v1.0.0 · C++17, JUCE 8, VST3

  • Mówik

    Push-to-talk dictation for Windows: hold F8, speak, release. Transcription runs on the PC.

    2026 · Python, faster-whisper

About

I am studying for a master’s in Sound Design at FH Joanneum in Graz, after a bachelor’s in Game Sound Integration at Howest DAE in Kortrijk. Before that I trained in classical piano and music theory at the State Music School in Kołobrzeg.

From February to June 2026 I was Technical Sound Designer at XR Labs in Brussels. Since 2026 I have also freelanced: sound design and a sound effects package for WeirdJohnny Studios, under NDA.

Besides Wwise, Unreal Engine and Unity, I work in FMOD, Pro Tools and Perforce, and I write C++, C#, Python and Lua.
