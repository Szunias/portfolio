Selected work

  • HUSHABYE

    A horror game played with a microphone, which I am making alone in Unity 6 with Wwise and my own C# DSP.

    I also do the game design, the C# gameplay and the models of the house and the creature. A real “shh” calms the baby, and your voice, recorded into baby-monitor cameras, lures a blind creature away.

    In production · 2026 · Unity 6 · C# · Wwise

  • Shadow Frames

    Sole audio designer on a pub horror game where changes are found by ear.

    I made the ambience that changes with every confirmed photo, footsteps on five surfaces, anomaly cues, the voice lines I recorded or edited, and a mix that reacts to the enemy.

    2025 · Wwise · UE5 · C++ · REAPER

  • XR Labs

    Weapon audio, Taser sounds and Steam Audio for police, military and customs VR training.

Tools

  • AutoTrim Exporter

    A REAPER tool I designed and built for delivering sound effects. It trims the silence around each sound and checks every render before delivery.

    2026 · Lua ReaScript · ReaImGui View the workflow →

  • AudioQ

    A C++/JUCE sampler: loop a recording, cut it into grains, crush and stutter it.

    v1.0.0 · C++17 · JUCE 8 · VST3

  • Mówik

    Push-to-talk dictation for Windows: hold F8, speak, release. Transcription runs on the PC.

    2026 · Python · faster-whisper

About

I studied Game Sound Integration at Howest DAE in Kortrijk and trained in classical piano and music theory at the State Music School in Kołobrzeg.

Besides Wwise, Unreal Engine and Unity, I work in FMOD, Pro Tools and Perforce, and I write C++, C#, Python and Lua.
