About

I’m a technical sound designer and audio programmer with a Game Sound Integration background at Howest DAE. At XR Labs, I implement audio for Unity VR/AR training. I focus on systems that change with gameplay rather than linear audio alone.

I am based in Kortrijk, Belgium, with a background in twelve years of classical piano. I am open to junior technical sound design roles.
