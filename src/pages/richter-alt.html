Richter

2024 · Sound design and score · REAPER · Zoom H6 · field recording · synthesis

Richter is a short film about an earthquake.

I did the sound design and the score. I used no commercial sound libraries: everything in the soundtrack is either my own recording or a synthesizer, and I mixed all of it around the dialogue.

What the rumble is made of

Synthesizers carry the deep part of the earthquake. Over them sit stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes from my own recordings, so the ground sounds like it is breaking instead of holding one long low note.

The quake builds behind a craft crossing open flats: a ridge bursts apart behind it, and it runs on into a narrow canyon inside the dust.

The first mix hid the dialogue

Parts of the speech disappeared under the earthquake. I cut the rumble in the same frequency range as the voices and turned it down whenever the actors spoke, so the earthquake stays heavy and the lines still come through.
