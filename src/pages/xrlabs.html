Weapon audio and headset testing

The simulations are used in training for police, military and customs services. I built the weapon audio system and designed Taser T10 sounds from layered library recordings.

I tuned spatialisation in the headset, checking whether a source could be located by ear and how a wall changed its sound.

The hall and the simulated room, side by side.

Spatialisation and sound between rooms

I used Steam Audio for directional sources, occlusion and indirect paths. These stills from the public facility video show the room layouts; they do not demonstrate the production audio.

Weapon sound across distance

Each shot blends near and far layers. As the listener moves away, close detail fades while the distance layer takes over.
