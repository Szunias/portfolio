The audio I built into the simulations

At XR Labs Belgium I designed and implemented audio for selected simulations. I used spatialisation, occlusion and distance layers so trainees could locate a source, hear when a wall blocked it and judge how far away a weapon shot was.

The hall and the simulated room, side by side.

Direction and indirect paths between rooms

I kept sources fixed in the virtual room and used Steam Audio for direction, occlusion and indirect paths. The frames are stills from the public clip below.

Near and far layers across distance

Each shot blends near and far layers. As the listener moves away, close detail fades while the distance layer takes over.
