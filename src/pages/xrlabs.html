Technical Sound Designer. I built the weapon audio system and tuned Steam Audio spatialisation in the headset.

At XR Labs Belgium I worked on the audio of selected Unity VR training simulations used by police, military and customs services.

February to June 2026, Brussels. Unity, Steam Audio, Perforce.

I also designed the Taser T10 sounds from layered library recordings.

The video and the stills are public footage that XR Labs published on YouTube ↗. Client scenarios, production audio, code and internal settings are not shown.

One shot, four positions

The spatialisation had to let a trainee locate a gunshot by ear and hear when a wall stood in the way.

Close by

The shot keeps its place in the yard, so the trainee can turn towards it. My weapon audio blends a near and a far layer for every shot; this close, the near layer carries the detail.

Across the yard

Further from the shooter, the near layer fades and the far layer takes over.
