Akantilado

2024 · Sound design and mix · REAPER · foley · 5.1 · 4 months · Score by Noah Bots

Akantilado is a student animation about two cats in a jungle.

I did the sound on my own: more than 200 sounds assembled and edited, then a stereo mix and a 5.1 mix built around Noah Bots’s score.

Watch the film ↗

How the jungle changes with the camera

Five background layers move with the shot. Close shots bring forward insects, leaves and claws on bark. Wide shots open into wind, water and distant animals, and stay under the music.

Telling the two cats apart

I matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation. The orange cat has heavier footsteps and a lower voice, the pink cat is lighter and quicker, so the two are easy to tell apart.
