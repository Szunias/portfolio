IFMC Winter 2025

Music for two IFMC short films

I wrote replacement scores for two short films as entries to IFMC Winter 2025. The finished entries and my main music choices are below, along with the jury’s full review of When The Clapping Stops.

01 · Animation

Walter

Walter has no dialogue, so the music carries his concern, the plant’s decline and the relief when the room fills with seeds. The same musical idea connects the wind hitting the window with the plant’s final bloom.

02 · Live action

When The Clapping Stops

The film is driven by conversation, so I kept the music around the speech and removed it completely in the final hallway. The jury found the tension effective, but felt the music during the seizure was heavier than the character’s controlled response needed.

“The introduction of the heavy brass and high strings riff is really well done in the context of your score.”
