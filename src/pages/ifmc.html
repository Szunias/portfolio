IFMC Winter 2025 · Two replacement scores

Music for two IFMC short films

I composed replacement scores for two short films in the Indie Film Music Contest (IFMC) Winter 2025: the animation Walter and the live-action film When The Clapping Stops.

Walter has no dialogue. I used one recurring musical idea to connect the wind at the window, the plant’s decline and its final bloom.

When The Clapping Stops

The film runs on conversation, so I kept the music around the speech and took it out completely in the final hallway.

The juror’s written review praised the growing tension and the silent ending, but found the seizure cue too heavy for the character’s controlled response.

“The introduction of the heavy brass and high strings riff is really well done in the context of your score.”
