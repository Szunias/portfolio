IFMC Winter 2025

Two IFMC scores

Walter received 48/60 and When The Clapping Stops received 47/60. This page presents the films, my scoring decisions and the jurors’ written feedback.

01 · 48/60 · 58th of 115 in round one

Walter

With no dialogue, the score carries Walter’s concern, the plant’s decline and the release when the room fills with seed. The cue connects the wind battering the window with the plant’s final bloom.

“You connect the wind with the blooming of the plant which is very well done.”

IFMC juror feedback

02 · 47/60 · 15th of 30 in round one

When The Clapping Stops

The score builds around continuous dialogue and then leaves the final hallway dry. The jury found the tension effective but considered the seizure cue heavier than the character’s controlled response required.

“The introduction of the heavy brass and high strings riff is really well done in the context of your score.”
