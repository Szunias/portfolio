Two scores for the Indie Film Music Contest

For the IFMC Winter 2025 edition I composed replacement scores for two short films: Walter, an animation with no dialogue, and When The Clapping Stops, a live-action film that runs on conversation.

Walter

Animation

The music runs without a break from 0:06 to 3:45 and carries Walter’s concern, the plant’s decline and the relief when the room fills with seeds. One recurring idea connects the wind hitting the window with the plant’s final bloom.

When The Clapping Stops

Live action

Matty is June’s home carer. I kept the music around the speech. It turns a little warmer after he says “I like you now”, shifts into dark sound design when her seizure starts and brings in a heavy brass and high strings riff as the tension grows. From his decision to go to his grandfather through the montage, it keeps building as one sequence.
