Sole audio designer: sound design, voice recording, implementation and the mix, in Wwise and Unreal Engine 5.

A black-and-white horror game set in one abandoned pub. The player finds changes by ear and photographs them with a Polaroid camera.

2025, four months, at Howest DAE with students from RITCS and the Royal Conservatory of Ghent. Wwise, Unreal Engine 5, C++, REAPER. Over 200 Wwise events in the finished game.

The ambience follows the board

Every confirmed photograph goes on the board, and each one moves the pub’s ambience a stage further. The first adds weird noises to the base layer and the third a ghostly screech.

In Wwise it is a switch group on RTPC_Photos_OnBoard, with a stage for every count from 0 to 10.

The fourth swaps both for a horror drone, and from the eighth only the horror background ambience is left.

Footsteps on five surfaces

The player’s footsteps change with the floor: tiles, wood, concrete, creaky wood and creaky wooden stairs. The enemy’s footsteps cover the same five surfaces and come from its own Data Asset.

I exposed floor, anomaly and enemy settings as Unreal Data Assets, so designers could tune them without changing code.

BP_FootstepAudioManager listens to the player’s On Footstep event, sets the FootstepSurface switch for the surface each step reports, then posts Play_PLYR_Footstep.

Anomaly sounds, from alert to whisper

The player finds changes in the pub without on-screen markers, so every change has to be heard first.

  1. Alert

    A short alert with no direction signals that an object in the pub has changed.

  2. Object

    The changed object plays its own spatial sound, and the player follows it through the pub.

  3. Camera

    Raising the camera lowers the pub ambience and brings breathing and hand movement forward, in a mix state of its own.

  4. Whisper

    A reversed whisper confirms the shot.

Keiran’s voice lines

I recorded the voice lines. Keiran, the player character, has his own lines for the story anomalies, played from one switch container on the StoryAnomaly switch group. Four reminder lines, such as IshouldPickup, work the same way on a switch group of their own.

The mix around the enemy

The mix follows the enemy’s distance and which anomalies are active. When too many sounds play at once, music and ambience give way.

The clues, the camera and the enemy stay, because those are the sounds the player is acting on.

The enemy has its own Data Asset: dread layers that follow its proximity, and a breathing loop on the player’s stress level.

The dread layers start at one threshold (5.0) and stop at a lower one (2.0), so they hold steady while the enemy hovers near the edge.
