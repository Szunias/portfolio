Shadow Frames

Sole audio designer on a horror game navigated entirely by sound.

I created the anomaly cues, edited the assets, built the Wwise and Unreal systems and mixed the game. Players locate ten anomalies by ear inside an abandoned Irish pub.

4 months · Wwise · Unreal Engine 5 · C++ · Reaper · Howest DAE + RITCS + Royal Conservatory of Ghent

Gameplay

Ten anomalies located without HUD markers

An anomaly changes an object in the pub. A stinger announces the change, its spatial cue provides a direction and a close loop confirms the target before the player photographs it.

Gameplay · spatial mix

Audio behaviour

Anomaly cues, enemy distance and camera state

Weekly playtests exposed three unreliable behaviours. The enemy layer flickered at a single distance threshold, footsteps broke when level geometry moved, and raising the Polaroid did not read clearly enough in the mix.

I added separate enter and exit distances for the enemy layer, changed footsteps to read the physical material under the player, and made the camera state muffle the pub while bringing breathing and hand movement forward. A reversed whisper confirms each photograph.

Implementation

Designer controls and mix priorities

Surface, anomaly and enemy settings live in Unreal DataAssets. Designers could tune them without changing code.

The mix reacts to enemy distance, camera state and anomaly activity. Under the voice limit, music and ambience yield before camera, anomaly and enemy cues.
