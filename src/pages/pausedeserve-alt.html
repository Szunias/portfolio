Pause & Deserve

2024 · Solo development · Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · MetaSounds · 8 weeks

My first Unreal horror prototype: a chase in which pausing is part of the game.

I designed and built all of it alone: the game logic, the level, the sound effects and the audio implementation. The music is library material.

Watch on YouTube ↗

What pausing costs

Pausing freezes the enemy for five seconds. When the chase resumes the enemy is permanently faster, so every pause makes the rest of the run harder than it would otherwise have been.

Hearing the enemy off screen

The enemy spends part of the chase outside the frame, so the player has to place it by ear. Footsteps, breaking branches and movement sounds go through HRTF spatialisation, which keeps their direction readable on headphones. The footsteps say which side the enemy is on, the branches break in the undergrowth it moves through, and the movement sounds carry the rest of what it makes as it closes.
