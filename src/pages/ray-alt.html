Ray

2024 · Composer · Pro Tools · Logic Pro

Ray is a DAE graduation animation about a stone giant who discovers dance.

I came in as the composer and wrote the finished score: disco-funk, with slap bass, drums, brass and sax.

Music timed to Ray’s movement

I marked the key movements in the animation, then changed the pace of the music so that its strongest hits land on Ray’s actions.

Eight days before the screening

I joined the film eight days before its cinema screening. There were three rounds of feedback with the animation team, and I used them to tighten the timing and the arrangement. Tijn Valcke and Charlie Vervaeke created the film’s sound design.
