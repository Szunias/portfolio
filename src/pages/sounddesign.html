Selected audio redesigns

Sound Design Archive

I created these audio redesigns as the sound designer, using field recordings, synthesis and foley. The original footage is used only as picture; all audio in the clips is my replacement.

01 · Unofficial redesign

God of War - Combat

Original audio removed. Axe impacts combine recorded metal and meat layers, with a compressed transient for the hit and a longer metallic tail for the weapon.

02 · Unofficial redesign

Baldur’s Gate 3 - Spells

Fire begins with a pitched gas-burner recording, ice uses granular texture with a slower attack, and lightning uses a short, hard transient. Different envelopes make the spell type readable before the visual settles.

03 · Foley and synthesis

P2000 and sci-fi weapon

The P2000 is built from recorded mechanics, a dry close shot and added room reflections. The sci-fi weapon uses synthesis and spectral processing, with one recorded mechanical click to give the sound a physical start.
