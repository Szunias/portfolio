Two sound redesigns

A fight in God of War and three spells in Baldur’s Gate 3, with my own sound in place of the original.

Watch the fight with sound

I built the new sound from my own recordings, foley and synthesis.

Independent practice pieces. I did not work on either game.

God of War: the fight

  1. 0:04The grapple
  2. 0:06Punches
  3. 0:09The axe

A sharp transient marks the impact, and a longer metallic ring identifies the weapon.

I layered metal and meat recordings so the axe hits feel immediate and heavy.

Baldur’s Gate 3: spells

Each spell starts differently, so it is recognisable before the visual effect settles.

  1. 0:02Ice Storm

  3. 0:08Glyph of Warding: Fire
