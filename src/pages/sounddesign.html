Two sound redesigns

I removed the audio from two game clips and rebuilt it from my own recordings, foley and synthesis: three spells in Baldur’s Gate 3 and a fight in God of War.

Both are independent practice pieces. I did not work on either game.

Recording · Foley · Synthesis

Baldur’s Gate 3: spells

Each spell starts differently, so it is recognisable before the visual effect settles.

  1. 00:00:02:02Ice StormForms more slowly, with a grainy texture.
  2. 00:00:03:15LightningArrives as a short, hard crack.
  3. 00:00:08:07Glyph of Warding: FireGrows out of a reshaped gas-burner recording.

God of War: combat

I layered metal and meat recordings so the axe hits feel immediate and heavy.

  1. 00:00:04:18The grapple
