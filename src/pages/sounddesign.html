Three sound redesigns

Three short clips where I removed the original audio and replaced it with sounds I recorded, performed and made with synthesizers.

Every sound in them is mine, from the recording to the synthesis. The God of War and Baldur’s Gate 3 clips are unofficial redesigns.

Recording · Foley · Synthesis

The ice spell landing, in the Baldur’s Gate 3 clip.

God of War: combat

I wanted each axe hit to land heavy, so I layered recorded metal with recorded meat. A short, sharp front marks the impact and a longer metallic ring says what the weapon is.

Combat from God of War, with my audio.

Baldur’s Gate 3: spells

Each spell starts differently, so it is recognisable before the visual effect settles. Fire grows out of a reshaped gas-burner recording, ice forms more slowly with a grainy texture, and lightning arrives as a short, hard crack.

Spellcasting in Baldur’s Gate 3, with my audio.

P2000 and sci-fi weapon

The P2000 is recorded weapon mechanics, a dry close shot and reflections that place it inside a room. The sci-fi weapon comes mostly from electronically shaped sounds, but a real mechanical click gives it a solid, physical start.

The P2000 and the sci-fi weapon, both from foley and synthesis.
