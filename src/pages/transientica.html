Transientica

A rhythm game you play by beatboxing into a microphone. I am developing it solo in Unity 6 and wrote its audio recognition in C#: the game learns how each player makes a kick, a snare and a hi-hat, then reads every live hit as an input on one of three lanes.

2025–present · Solo · Unity 6 · C# · KNN · FFT · In development

From a sound to a lane

Before the first song, the player teaches the game their own sounds: at least ten recordings each of their kick, snare and hi-hat, made with their own microphone. The game measures every recording and saves the measurements, labelled by sound, in a JSON file, so a player can retrain between rounds.

I wrote the recognition in C#, including the FFT and a k-nearest-neighbours (KNN) classifier, so it runs inside the game with no Python or second program beside it.

Every hit during a song

  1. OnsetThe microphone registers a hit.
  2. FFTA 512-sample FFT splits the hit into its frequencies.
  3. FeaturesSeven numbers are measured from the hit, the same seven that were saved for each training recording.
  4. KNNThe training recordings with the closest numbers decide which sound it was.
  5. LaneThe game names the sound at the top of the screen, and the hit becomes an input on that lane.Kick Snare Hi-hat

Judged on the audio clock

Each hit is matched to the nearest note on Unity’s audio clock and scored by its timing. A short calibration estimates the microphone delay, and the offset can be adjusted in 1 ms steps.

Around the recognition I built the rest of the game: song selection, beatmaps, scoring and saved results.

The 2025 Python prototype

The first version was a Python script. It found each hit in the microphone signal with onset detection, classified it with a machine learning model I had trained to recognise three beatbox sounds, and sent the result to Unity over OSC, where the lane for that sound reacted.

The current build is the game I planned at the end of the prototype’s video: a proper rhythm game where you have to beatbox in sync with the beat to score points.
