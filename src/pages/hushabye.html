Singing to the crib

When TALK blinks green, hold it and sing into your microphone. YIN pitch detection runs in real time and only on the player’s PC; the voice is never sent anywhere.

A phrase judge scores every note against the melody in the singer’s own key, which their first steady note sets.

When the monitor goes dark and the breathing stops, it is listening: freeze and stay silent.

To get out, find the front-door key and turn it in the lock before the parents come home at six.

Sound through walls and floors

I built a C# DSP engine that carries sound through walls, floors and doorways along a graph of the house’s zones. Each zone type has one of five FDN reverbs: room, kitchen, corridor, tiled bathroom and a dead pantry.

A sound with hiss is far away, and one without it is right next to you.

The monitor’s speaker is a lo-fi mono chain with hiss; the house around the player is full band.

The music box plays retuned glockenspiel samples from VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0) with a shorter decay, a 450 Hz high-pass, teeth detuned by ±3 cents and the whirr of the mechanism.
