Amorak

Amorak is a two-minute horror animation. I redesigned its audio independently and replaced the original soundtrack.

Four animals in one voice

I combined a lion for the low growl, an elephant for weight, a tiger for the snarl and a hippo for its rough edge. I shaped each layer separately and made more than fifteen versions to match what the creature does on screen.

The whole two-minute scene with the new soundtrack. Headphones help.

What overlaps in the basement

The creature and the basement sounds move around the listener. Breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap.

Making room for the breathing

When breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap, I lowered the competing sounds enough to keep the breathing clear.
