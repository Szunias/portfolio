2024 · Horror animation redesign

Amorak

Amorak is a horror animation. For this independent sound redesign, I replaced the soundtrack with a layered creature voice and a binaural mix for headphones.

Sound designer · 4 weeks · REAPER · dearVR PRO · Krotos Studio

Watch on YouTube ↗

Four animals in one voice

I combined a lion for the low growl, an elephant for weight, a tiger for the snarl and a hippo for its rough edge. I shaped each layer separately and made more than fifteen versions to match what the creature does on screen.

The complete two-minute redesign. Headphones recommended for the binaural mix.

Binaural placement and breathing

I used dearVR PRO to place the creature and basement sounds around the listener. When breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap, I lowered the competing layers to keep the breathing audible.
