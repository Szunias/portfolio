Amorak is a two-minute horror animation. In this sound redesign I built the creature’s voice from four animals and placed the creature and the basement sounds around the listener in a binaural mix for headphones.

2024 · Independent redesign · 4 weeks ·
REAPER · dearVR PRO · Krotos Studio

Also on YouTube ↗

The creature’s voice

I combined a lion for the low growl, an elephant for weight, a tiger for the snarl and a hippo for its rough edge.

I shaped each layer separately and made more than fifteen versions to match what the creature does on screen.

The basement around the listener

I did the binaural placement in dearVR PRO. When breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap, I lowered the competing layers to keep the breathing audible.

Amorak was made at Digital Arts and Entertainment, Howest, by Arnold De Rauw, Ward Dermaut, Maarten Leys, Jitse Peeraer, Aderik Van Thorre and Arvid Van Thorre. The end credits in the video are the film’s own and name its original sound design and music team.
