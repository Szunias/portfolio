My independent sound redesign of a two‑minute horror animation.

Watch the film with sound Also on YouTube ↗

I gave the creature a new voice and placed it and the basement in a binaural mix for headphones.

2024, four weeks. REAPER, dearVR PRO, Krotos Studio.

The creature’s voice

I combined a lion for the low growl, an elephant for weight, a tiger for the snarl and a hippo for its rough edge.

I shaped each layer separately and made more than fifteen versions to match what the creature does on screen. Hear it from 0:25.

The basement around the listener

I placed the creature and the basement around the listener in dearVR PRO. Where breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap, I lowered the competing layers so the breathing stays audible.

Hear the chains at 0:42 and the hatch at 0:47.

Amorak was made at Digital Arts and Entertainment, Howest, by Arnold De Rauw, Ward Dermaut, Maarten Leys, Jitse Peeraer, Aderik Van Thorre and Arvid Van Thorre. The end credits in the video are the film’s own and name its original sound design and music team.
