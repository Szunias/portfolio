Independent binaural redesign of a 90-second horror animation. I replaced the original soundtrack, built the creature voice, placed its movement around the listener and mixed the full scene for headphones.

Reaper · Dear VR Pro · Krotos Studio

Final redesign

Creature voice and binaural mix

Lion provides the low end, elephant adds mass, tiger supplies the upper snarl and hippo adds rasp. I processed the layers separately and cut more than fifteen variations to picture.

Creature movement and basement details surround the listener. Sidechain ducking keeps the character’s breathing audible when wood, chains and the roar overlap.
