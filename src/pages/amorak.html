Creature voice

Four animals, more than fifteen variations

Lion carries the low end, elephant adds mass, tiger supplies the upper snarl and hippo adds rasp. I pitched and stretched the layers separately, then cut more than 15 variations to picture.

Binaural mix

Movement around the listener

Creature movement and basement details are placed around the listener. Sidechain ducking keeps the character’s breathing audible when wood, chains and the roar overlap.
