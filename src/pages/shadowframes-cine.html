01 · The work

Every sound in the pub was mine

I was the only person on audio. I made the sounds that announce each change and lead the player to it, recorded the voice acting, then implemented and mixed all of it as 200+ Wwise events in Unreal.

A collaboration between Howest DAE, RITCS and the Royal Conservatory of Ghent.

How a change is heard

No bearing Locatable On photograph

01

Something in the room changed

A short alert reaches you. It carries no direction: it says that one of the ten things is different, not which one and not where.

02

Now it has a bearing

The changed object starts sounding for itself. From here the sound is the marker, and you can walk towards it.

03

Photograph it

Standing at the object you raise the Polaroid. The pub drops back, your breathing and hand movement come forward, and a reversed whisper confirms the shot.

02 · Weekly playtests

Three things the tests kept catching

The enemy sound switched on and off near one particular distance. Footsteps failed when parts of the level moved. And raising the Polaroid camera was easy to miss.

I stopped the enemy sound from flickering, made footsteps follow the actual floor material, and gave the raised camera its own mix state, which is the one you heard in the last step.
