Pause & Deserve

My first Unreal project: a solo-built horror prototype where each pause freezes the enemy for five seconds and permanently increases its speed.

Blueprints · MetaSounds · HRTF spatialisation

Gameplay

Pause mechanic and off-screen enemy cues

Short-term safety makes the next chase harder, so players have to ration pauses. Footsteps, branch cracks and movement cues help track the enemy when it is outside the frame.

I built the code, level and audio. Library music is credited separately; my work on this page is the implementation and sound effects.
