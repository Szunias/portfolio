Pause & Deserve

My first Unreal horror prototype: a chase in which pausing is part of the game.

Solo development · 8 weeks · Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · MetaSounds

Watch on YouTube ↗

What pausing costs

Pausing freezes the enemy for five seconds. When the chase resumes it is permanently faster, so every pause makes the rest of the run harder.

The game states the rule in its own handwriting.

Everything but the music is mine

I designed and built all of it alone: the game logic, the level, the sound effects and the audio implementation. The music is library material.

Gameplay from the prototype, with the spatial mix.

Hearing the enemy off screen

The enemy spends part of the chase outside the frame, so the player has to place it by ear. Footsteps, breaking branches and movement sounds go through HRTF spatialisation, which keeps their direction readable on headphones.
