A horror prototype I made alone in Unreal Engine 5, with my own sound effects.

Watch the trailer with sound Also on YouTube ↗

Death chases you through a red-lit corridor and the undergrowth. Pressing P freezes it for five seconds, and every pause leaves it permanently faster.

2024, on my own, in eight weeks. Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints, MetaSounds. The music is from a library.

Death’s footsteps in HRTF

On headphones you can place Death while it is off screen.

Its footsteps, breaking branches and movement are spatialised with HRTF.

I made the sound effects for the game and implemented them in Unreal with MetaSounds.

P pauses Death

I built the chase and the pause rule in Blueprints. In the trailer the rule appears as a hint just before the chase.
