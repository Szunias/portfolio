Pause & Deserve

Pause & Deserve is my first Unreal horror prototype. I designed and built it alone: pausing freezes the enemy for five seconds, but permanently makes it faster when the chase resumes.

Blueprints · MetaSounds · HRTF spatialisation

Gameplay

Pausing buys time, then makes the chase harder

Each pause offers immediate safety at a permanent cost, so the player cannot rely on it forever. Footsteps, breaking branches and movement sounds reveal where the enemy is even when it is outside the frame.

I built the game logic, level and audio. The library music is credited separately; my audio work here is the sound effects and their implementation in the game.
