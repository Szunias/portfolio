Pause & Deserve

A horror prototype I made on my own in Unreal Engine 5 in eight weeks, sound effects included. Death’s footsteps, breaking branches and movement are spatialised with HRTF, so on headphones you can place it while it is off screen. Pressing P freezes Death for five seconds, and every pause leaves it permanently faster.
