A horror prototype I made alone in Unreal Engine 5: the level, the game logic and the sound effects.

Watch the trailer with sound Also on YouTube ↗

Death chases you through a red-lit corridor and the undergrowth. Pressing P freezes it for five seconds, and every pause leaves it permanently faster.

2024, eight weeks. Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints, MetaSounds. The music is from a library.

Death’s footsteps in HRTF

Death’s steps, the branches it breaks and its movement are spatialised with HRTF, so on headphones you can place it while it is off screen. I implemented the sound effects in Unreal with MetaSounds.

P pauses Death

I built the chase and the pause rule in Blueprints.
