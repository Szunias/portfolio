Amorak

2024 · Sound designer · REAPER · dearVR PRO · Krotos Studio · 4 weeks

Amorak is a two-minute horror animation. I redesigned its audio independently and replaced the original soundtrack.

I was the sound designer. I built the creature’s voice and mixed the scene so that on headphones the creature travels around the listener.

Watch on YouTube ↗

The creature’s voice

I combined four animals: a lion for the low growl, an elephant for weight, a tiger for the snarl and a hippo for its rough edge. I shaped each layer separately and made more than fifteen versions to match what the creature does on screen.

What moves around the listener

The creature and the basement sounds move around the listener. The scene stays in one basement: chains with sides of meat hanging from the ceiling, a wooden hatch the creature is seen through from below, and the stairs behind its roar. When breathing, wood, chains and the roar overlap, I lowered the competing sounds enough to keep the breathing clear.
