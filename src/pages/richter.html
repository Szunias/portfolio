Richter

Richter is a short film about an earthquake. I wrote its score and made every sound effect in it myself, from recordings I took with a Zoom H6 on walks through the city and from synthesizers, without any sound libraries.

Year
2024
Role
Score, sound design, mix
Tools
REAPER, Zoom H6, Vital, ReaSynth, TAL-NoiseMaker

Cue by cue

Vital, ReaSynth and TAL-NoiseMaker gave me the low end of the quake, and stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes from my recordings became the materials breaking above the rumble.

  1. The rumble

    A low rumble starts to swell under the plain, seven seconds before the ground on screen splits.

  2. The craft

    The craft races across the flats, and the main theme comes in under its engines.

  3. The ground splits

    The earth cracks over the rumble as the fault runs across the plain.

  4. The collapse

    The ridge bursts apart behind the craft, and the low end reaches its peak for the whole film.

  5. Closing gate

    A gate shuts on the valley, then the sound drops to almost nothing for three seconds.

  6. Title hit

    The title lands on a single hit, over the last bars of the theme.

Every sound I made for the film went into my own library, named and tagged.
