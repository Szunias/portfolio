Score, sound design and mix for a short film about an earthquake.

Watch the film with sound

I made every sound effect in it myself, from recordings I took with a Zoom H6 on walks through the city and from synthesizers, without any sound libraries.

2024. Score, sound design and mix. REAPER, Zoom H6, Vital, ReaSynth, TAL-NoiseMaker.

Cue by cue

  1. 0:03The rumble

    A low rumble swells under the plain, seven seconds before the ground splits.

  2. 0:07The craft

    The craft races across the flats, and the main theme comes in under its engines.

  3. 0:10The ground splits

    The earth cracks over the rumble as the fault runs across the plain.

  4. 0:18The collapse

    The ridge bursts apart behind the craft, and the low end reaches its peak for the whole film.

  5. 0:28Closing gate

    A gate shuts on the valley, then the sound drops to almost nothing for three seconds.

  6. 0:37Title hit

    The title lands on a single hit, over the last bars of the theme.

A quake from synths and city recordings

Stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes from my recordings became the materials breaking above the rumble.

Vital, ReaSynth and TAL-NoiseMaker gave me the low end of the quake.

Every sound I made for the film went into my own library, named and tagged.
