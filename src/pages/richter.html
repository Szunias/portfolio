Richter

Richter is a short film about an earthquake. I created its sound design and score without commercial sound libraries, using my own recordings and synthesizers before mixing everything around the dialogue.

REAPER · field recording · synthesis

Final film

A heavy earthquake with clear dialogue

Synthesizers provide the deep rumble. Stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes make the earthquake feel physical instead of sounding like one continuous low note.

The first mix hid parts of the speech. I reduced the rumble in the same frequency range as the voices and lowered it whenever the actors spoke, keeping the earthquake heavy without losing their lines.
