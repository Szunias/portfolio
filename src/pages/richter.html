Richter

Sound design and score for an earthquake short made without commercial libraries. I recorded the foley, built the low end from free synths and automated the mix to keep dialogue clear.

Final film

An earthquake built from recordings and synthesis

Building the earthquake

Low drones plus physical detail

Low drones from Vital and ReaSynth provide the weight. Stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes add the physical detail that a pure sub rumble lacked.

Dialogue

Making space inside the rumble

The first mix masked speech. I automated an EQ pocket around the dialogue range and ducked the rumble when actors spoke, keeping the quake heavy without losing the lines.
