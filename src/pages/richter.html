Richter

Sound design and score for an earthquake short made without commercial libraries. I recorded the foley, synthesized the low end and mixed the scene around the dialogue.

Final film

Earthquake weight without masking dialogue

Low drones from Vital and ReaSynth provide the weight. Stretched door creaks, pallet impacts and concrete scrapes add physical detail that a pure sub rumble lacked.

The first mix covered speech. I automated an EQ pocket around the dialogue range and ducked the rumble when actors spoke, keeping the quake heavy without losing the lines.
