A couch kart racer for up to four players: grannies on mobility scooters race over a course built from household objects in a living room.

2025, twelve weeks at Howest DAE, a six-person student team. Unreal Engine 5, MetaSounds, Blueprints, REAPER.

MetaSounds engines and pickups

Each kart runs a separate engine sound driven by its speed, throttle and load. I smoothed fast control changes, so the pitch follows the kart without jumping. Hear it from 0:58.

All 17 folders in Audio/Sounds ↗

Pickups and power-ups share one MetaSound patch, MSP_MonoArray. Each time it plays, it picks a file from its array, never the same one twice in a row, at a random pitch within a set range.

MS_Pickup layers two of these players into one stereo mix; MS_Powerups feeds a single one with CreamPiePickupSounds and also writes it to the AB_Ducker audio bus.

Mixing four players on one screen

I sorted the sounds into three priority tiers with concurrency limits. In a pile-up, kart feedback, major crashes and pickups keep priority, and distant engines and minor sounds give way first.

Master, Music, SFX and Voices each have a Sound Class and a class mix. Distance falloff comes from four shared attenuation presets: characters, deaths, ghosts and jump pads.

Jam slows a kart down and fans push it on. A zone plays its sound once, when the first granny enters, however many follow, and fades it out when the last one leaves. Hear the fans from 1:16.

The rest of the mix ducks under the commentator’s lines.

The grannies, voiced by the team

I recorded the team performing the grannies. Each one has a line when she is picked and another when she wins. The game varies the recordings and leaves time between reactions to limit repetition.
