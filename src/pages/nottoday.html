Audio implementation

MetaSounds engines and mix priorities

RPM, throttle and load drive each engine. Interpolation keeps fast input changes from producing abrupt pitch jumps.

The first mix gave every kart equal weight, so players lost their own engine in pile-ups. The final hierarchy keeps the local kart above critical impacts and pickups, with rival karts lower. Separate concurrency limits make lower-priority events yield first.

In the final week I recorded five teammates performing reactions for four characters. Randomised Sound Cues and cooldowns reduce repeated lines during a race.
