Keeping the local kart audible

Four engines, power-ups, collisions and character voices can fire in the same room.

My first mix gave every kart equal weight, and players lost their own engine in pile-ups.

The final hierarchy keeps the local kart on top, gameplay-critical impacts and pickups underneath, and rival karts lower in the mix. Each tier has its own concurrency limit, so lower-priority events give way before the player’s own engine disappears.
