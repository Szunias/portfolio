A first-person horror project I’m developing solo in Unreal Engine 5, from C++ gameplay to Wwise audio.

Watch the teaser with sound

Black Zones hide the room around the player. Each press of the scan button sends out a sound pulse that reveals nearby surfaces as points.

Scanning a Black Zone

I wrote the scanner in C++. The points it draws give the player a route through the darkness.

Prototype 0.4 is a 54-second recording of the scanner in a corridor blockout.

Null and the cost of a scan

Null is the enemy I’m designing. It will follow the player’s scans.

A more detailed scan would reveal a clearer route while making the player easier to track.

A sound somewhere else is meant to work as bait. I also plan to let microphone input and environmental sounds reveal surfaces, so the scan button is not the only way to see.
