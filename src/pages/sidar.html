SIDAR NULL

A first-person horror project I’m developing solo in Unreal Engine 5, from C++ gameplay to Wwise audio. I built a scanner that uses sound pulses to reveal dark rooms.

Scanning a Black Zone

Black Zones hide the room around the player. I wrote the scanner in C++: each press of the scan button sends out a sound pulse, and the pulse reveals nearby surfaces as points that give the player a route through the darkness.

Null and the cost of a scan

Null is the enemy I’m designing. It will follow the player’s scans, so a more detailed scan would reveal a clearer route while making the player easier to track. A sound somewhere else is meant to work as bait. I also plan to use microphone input and environmental sounds to reveal surfaces, so the scan button is not the only way to see.
