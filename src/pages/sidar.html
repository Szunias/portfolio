SIDAR NULL

A first-person horror project I’m developing solo in Unreal Engine 5, from C++ gameplay to Wwise audio. I built a scanner that uses sound pulses to reveal dark rooms.

Prototype recording

Navigating
by sound.

Black Zones hide the room around the player. Pressing the scan button reveals nearby surfaces as points, giving the player a route through the darkness.

This earlier recording shows the button-triggered scanner and corridor blockout in prototype 0.4.

In development

Pursuit and
sound sources.

The planned enemy, Null, will follow the player’s scans. A more detailed scan would reveal a clearer route while making the player easier to track. Sound placed elsewhere is intended to work as a distraction.

I also plan to use microphone input and environmental sounds to reveal surfaces. These behaviours are part of the design; the prototype 0.4 recording shows button-triggered scanning.
