SIDAR NULL

SIDAR NULL is a first-person exploration horror game currently in development. In the Black Zones, sound is the only reliable way to understand the space, but player-made pulses can also give Null a trail to follow.

Core mechanic

Sound reveals the room, and can leave Null a trail

In the vertical-slice design, the player reveals a dark room with a button press, a microphone or sounds already in the environment. Different scan profiles expose different parts of the space, so the player must decide how much information is actually needed.

The intended trade-off is simple: a detailed map is useful, but dangerous. Null is drawn to areas the system understands well, while a sound elsewhere can be used as bait before the player moves on.

Current statusActive vertical-slice prototype. The scanner, Black Zones, perception map and core C++ gameplay systems are in place; the level, final visuals and audio are still in development.
