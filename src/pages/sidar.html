SIDAR NULL

A first-person horror prototype I am developing solo in Unreal Engine. I built a scanner that uses sound pulses to reveal the geometry of dark rooms.

Game design, C++ gameplay systems and audio · Unreal Engine 5 · Wwise · Prototype 0.3

Prototype 0.3: scanning and navigating the corridor blockout.

Navigating with the scanner

Black Zones hide the room around the player. Pressing the scan button reveals nearby surfaces as points, giving the player a route through the darkness.

Planned pursuit and sound sources

The planned enemy, Null, will follow the player’s scans. A more detailed scan would reveal a clearer route to the player while making them easier to track. Sound placed elsewhere is intended to work as a distraction.

I also plan to use microphone input and environmental sounds to reveal surfaces. These behaviours are part of the design; the recording above shows the button-triggered scanner.
