Composer · 2024

Ray

Ray is a DAE graduation animation about a stone giant who wakes in a cave and discovers dance. I joined eight days before its cinema screening and delivered the disco-funk score.

Pro Tools · Logic Pro

Final film

Tempo-mapped score for the cinema screening

Slap bass, drums, brass and sax carry the score. I placed sync markers in Pro Tools and adjusted the tempo around Ray’s movement so major musical hits land with the animation.

Three feedback rounds with the animation team tightened the timing and arrangement before the screening. The film credits Tijn Valcke and Charlie Vervaeke for sound design.
