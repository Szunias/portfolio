Ray

I composed the disco-funk score in the eight days before the film’s cinema screening.

Watch the film with sound Ray on YouTube ↗

A student animation about a stone giant who discovers dance, scored for slap bass, drums, brass and sax.

2024, Howest DAE. 2:28 film. Composer. Logic Pro, with sample libraries from Splice.

  1. 0:09Alone in the cave
  2. 0:55First moves
  3. 1:08Light in the cave
  4. 1:16Arms out
  5. 1:29Hand in the beam

Timing the score to Ray’s moves

I marked Ray’s key movements in the animation and put the strongest bass, brass and percussion hits on them.

Three rounds of feedback with the animation team tightened the timing and the arrangement before the screening.
