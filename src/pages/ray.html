2024 · DAE graduation animation

Ray

Ray is a DAE graduation animation about a stone giant who discovers dance. I composed its disco-funk score in the eight days before the cinema screening.

Composer · Pro Tools · Logic Pro

Disco-funk for a stone giant

Ray mid-dance in the cave.

Music timed to Ray’s movement

I marked the key movements in the animation, then changed the pace of the music so that its strongest hits land on Ray’s actions.

The finished film with the score.

Eight days before the screening

There were three rounds of feedback with the animation team in those eight days, and I used them to tighten the timing and the arrangement. Tijn Valcke and Charlie Vervaeke created the film’s sound design.
