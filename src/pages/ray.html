Ray

Ray is a Howest DAE student animation about a stone giant who discovers dance. I composed its disco-funk score for slap bass, drums, brass and sax in the eight days before the film’s cinema screening.

2024 · Composer · Pro Tools · Logic Pro

0:00 / 2:28

Timing the score to Ray

I marked Ray’s key movements in the animation and timed the bass, brass and percussion so the strongest hits land on his moves. Three rounds of feedback with the animation team tightened the timing and the arrangement.
