Akantilado

Sole sound designer on a 3D jungle short. I recorded and edited more than 200 sounds, then delivered stereo and 5.1 mixes around Noah Bots’ score.

Final film

Jungle ambience, character foley and delivery

Five ambience layers follow the camera: close shots favour insects, leaves and claws on bark; wide shots open into wind, water and distant calls without covering the music.

Paw steps, branch grips and plant movement were synced to picture. Heavier footsteps and a lower voice distinguish the orange character from the lighter, quicker pink character. I handled sound design, foley, ambience and both mixes; Noah Bots composed the score.
