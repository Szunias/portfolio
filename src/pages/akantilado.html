Akantilado

Akantilado is a student animation about two cats in a jungle. As its sound designer and mixer, I created the creature sounds and jungle ambience, then delivered stereo and 5.1 mixes.

Sound design and mix · 4 months · REAPER · foley · 5.1 · Score by Noah Bots

Watch the film ↗

Sound design around the score

I assembled and edited more than 200 sounds for the film. Noah Bots composed the score; I handled the remaining audio and both mixes.

The finished film with my sound design and mix.

Ambience follows the camera

Five background layers move with the shot. Close shots bring forward insects, leaves and claws on bark. Wide shots open into wind, water and distant animals, and stay under the music.

Wide shots leave more room for the surrounding jungle.

Telling the two cats apart

I matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation, and separated the two cats by weight so they are easy to tell apart.
