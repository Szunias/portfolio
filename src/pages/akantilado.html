Akantilado

Sole sound designer on a 3D jungle short. I recorded and edited more than 200 sounds, then delivered stereo and 5.1 mixes around Noah Bots’ score.

Final film

Camera perspective drives the jungle

Ambience

Five layers shift with the camera

Close shots favour insects, leaves and claws on bark; wide shots open into wind, water and distant calls without covering the score.

Foley and delivery

Two characters with different weight

Paw steps, branch grips and plant movement were synced to picture. The orange character uses heavier footfalls and a lower voice; the pink character is lighter and quicker.

I handled sound design, foley, ambience and both mixes. Noah Bots composed the score.
