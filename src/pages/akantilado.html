Akantilado

Akantilado is a student animation about two cats in a jungle. I did the sound on my own.

Sound design and mix · 4 months · REAPER · foley · 5.1 · Score by Noah Bots

Watch the film ↗

More than 200 sounds, then two mixes

I assembled and edited more than 200 sounds, then built a stereo mix and a 5.1 mix around Noah Bots’s score. Everything in the film’s audio apart from that score is mine.

The finished film, with the sound I made for it.

How the jungle changes with the camera

Five background layers move with the shot. Close shots bring forward insects, leaves and claws on bark. Wide shots open into wind, water and distant animals, and stay under the music.

A wide shot: the jungle opens out and the cats are small in it.

Telling the two cats apart

I matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation, and separated the two cats by weight so they are easy to tell apart.
