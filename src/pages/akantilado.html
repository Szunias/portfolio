Akantilado

Akantilado is a student animation about two cats in a jungle. I was its sound designer and mixer: apart from Noah Bots’s score, everything you hear is mine. I assembled and edited more than 200 sounds for the cats and the jungle, and made the stereo and 5.1 mixes.

2024 · 4 months · REAPER · Foley · Stereo and 5.1

Ambience follows the camera

I built the jungle from five background layers that move with the shot, and matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation. Close shots bring insects, leaves and claws on bark forward.
