Akantilado

Akantilado is a student jungle animation about two cats. As the sole sound designer, I assembled and edited more than 200 sounds, then delivered stereo and surround mixes around Noah Bots’ score.

Final film

Making the jungle and characters easy to follow

Five background layers change with the camera. Close shots bring forward insects, leaves and claws on bark; wide shots open into wind, water and distant animals without covering the music.

I matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation. Heavier footsteps and a lower voice separate the orange cat from the lighter, quicker pink cat. Noah Bots composed the score; I handled all other audio and both final mixes.
