A student animation about two cats in a jungle. I assembled and edited more than 200 sounds for the cats and the jungle, and made the stereo and 5.1 mixes.

2024, four months. REAPER and Foley.

Ambience follows the camera

I built the jungle from five background layers that move with the shot.

The orange cat and the pink cat

I matched paw steps, branch grips and moving plants to the animation.

I gave the orange cat heavier steps and a lower voice, and the pink cat lighter steps and quicker vocalisations.
