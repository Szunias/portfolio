AudioQ

A desktop audio tool and plug-in for loading a recording, choosing a loop and reshaping it with effects.

I am the solo developer. I built the player, waveform editor and processing chain in C++. All 16 controls are saved with the plug-in state and available to host automation.

C++17 · JUCE 8 · Standalone and VST3 · MIT

One stateful chain

The interface follows the order of the sound: select the material, shape the loop, process it and blend the result.

  1. File or loop
  2. Source playback
  3. Filters
  4. Compressor
  5. Movement and glitch
  6. Dry / wet blend

How it works

AudioQ loads WAV, AIFF and MP3 files. The user drags across the waveform to choose a loop, smooths its join with a crossfade and can resample the material between 20 and 300 BPM.

The sound then passes through filters, a compressor, tremolo, bitcrusher, stutter and reverse effects. JUCE supplies the filters and compressor; I wrote the remaining effects.

Current limits

The compressor handles normal peaks. If the signal still exceeds 4.0 linear gain, a separate fail-safe stops playback. It is a last stop, not certified hearing protection.

Full host, operating-system, sample-rate and channel-layout testing is still pending.
