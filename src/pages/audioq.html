AudioQ

A desktop audio tool and VST3 plug-in for loading a recording, looping it and reshaping it with effects.

As the solo developer, I built the player, waveform editor and full processing chain in C++. Its 16 controls can be saved and automated inside music software.

Implementation

How a loaded sound is processed and saved

Loaded sample or loop → small overlapping grains → filters → compressor → movement and glitch effects → original/effected blend.

Users can drop in a WAV, AIFF or MP3 file, drag across its waveform to choose a loop and smooth the join with a crossfade. They can also resample it between 20 and 300 BPM.

The sound passes through filters, a compressor, tremolo, bitcrusher, stutter and reverse effects. JUCE provides the filters and compressor; I wrote the remaining effects. The plug-in saves every control and exposes it to the host for automation.

Overlapping grains can cause sudden volume peaks. The compressor reduces normal peaks; if the signal still exceeds 4.0 linear gain, a separate fail-safe stops playback.

The stop is not certified hearing protection. Full host, operating-system, sample-rate and channel-layout testing is still pending.
