AudioQ

A desktop audio tool and VST3 plug-in for loading a recording, selecting a loop and reshaping it with effects.

I am the solo developer. I built the player, waveform editor and processing chain in C++. Its 16 controls can be saved and automated inside music software.

C++17 · JUCE 8 · Standalone and VST3 · MIT

02

Signal path

A loaded sound passes through one stateful processing chain

The interface follows the order in which the user works: choose material, shape the loop, process it and blend the result.

File or loop Source playback Filters Compressor Movement and glitch Dry / wet blend

Users can load a WAV, AIFF or MP3 file, drag across its waveform to choose a loop and smooth the join with a crossfade. They can also resample it between 20 and 300 BPM.

The sound passes through filters, a compressor, tremolo, bitcrusher, stutter and reverse effects. JUCE provides the filters and compressor; I wrote the remaining effects.

The plug-in saves every control and exposes it to the host for automation.

03

Limits

The fail-safe is a last stop, not hearing protection

A heavily processed signal can create sudden peaks. The compressor handles normal peaks; if the signal still exceeds 4.0 linear gain, a separate fail-safe stops playback.

The stop is not certified hearing protection. Full host, operating-system, sample-rate and channel-layout testing is still pending.
