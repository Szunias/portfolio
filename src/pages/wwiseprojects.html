Playback priority in Boney Boogaloo

Nearby weapons keep playing and distant enemy voices drop out first.

In the test scene, skeletons in suits keep rising over the hill, and more sounds play at once than the project’s voice limit allows. I set the playback priorities that decide what you still hear.

The skeletons crowd the hill from 1:04 in the recording.

Ellen, from the forest into the cave

The two ambiences crossfade and the reverb changes with them instead of switching in one step.

I also set the attenuation for each type of sound, which decides how quickly it fades with distance.

Stomach Aches on low-memory phones

I grouped its sounds by area, and the game loads only what it currently needs.

The Android build of Stomach Aches had to fit low-memory phones. I also replaced the audio files that only held silence with the Wwise Silence plug-in, so the gaps no longer cost memory.
