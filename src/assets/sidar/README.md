# SIDAR trailer poster

The poster comes from the YouTube thumbnail for Igor's **Annex04 Cinematic Teaser**, supplied for the SIDAR project page.

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nBWFn8nHhCI
- Original thumbnail: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/nBWFn8nHhCI/maxresdefault.jpg
- Retrieved: 6 September 2026
- Source: `annex04-cinematic-teaser-source.jpg`, 1280 × 720.
- Visible image: 1280 × 536, starting at x = 0, y = 92. The derivatives remove only the black letterbox bars.
- WebP derivatives: 1200 × 502 and 640 × 268, encoded at quality 88 with Lanczos downsampling. Neither image is upscaled or retouched.

The image shows the ANNEX / 04 facility and its green CRT terminals. It illustrates the cinematic teaser, not the older scanner prototype recording.
