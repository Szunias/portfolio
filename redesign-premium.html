About

Between the DAW and the engine

Most teams have someone who makes sounds, or someone who writes code. I do the part in between.

Production on one side: recording, mixing, composition. Implementation on the other: Wwise, FMOD, C++ and Blueprints in Unreal and Unity. My Game Sound Integration training at Digital Arts and Entertainment covered the full chain, from DAW to engine.

At XR Labs I do technical sound design for VR/AR training simulations used in defence and public safety. If a threat is behind a wall, it has to sound filtered, distant, and correctly placed.

Before any of this: twelve years of classical piano. It still shows up in how I hear a mix.
