Boney Boogaloo

Wwise v2021 Windows 2 weeks

Zombie wave shooter. 50 zombies on screen groaning, player firing constantly, ambient music, environment sounds, UI feedback all at once.

Hit play and half the audio cuts out. Wwise has a 256 voice limit - go over that and it starts killing sounds. Important stuff like zombie spawns and low ammo warnings get culled because background loops are using up all the voices.

Need to stay under 256 but also can't have the same gunshot repeat 100 times because that sounds like garbage.

10 containers
44 files
256 voice limit
6 variations

Fixed it with random containers - every gunshot picks from 6 different variations, zombie sounds randomize between groans and shuffles. Set up voice virtualization so quiet sounds pause instead of dying completely.

Also trimmed silence from every file manually. That half-second of nothing at the start of a reload sound? Gone. Zombie groan tail that's just room noise? Cut. Files load faster and use less memory.

Set culling threshold at -80 dB thinking quiet = safe to cut. Wrong. Zombie spawns got culled mid-wave because they start quiet and ramp up. Players couldn't hear threats spawning behind them. Had to bump it to -60 dB and manually rebalance everything.

Ellen

Wwise v2021 Windows 3 weeks

Third-person action game. Three zones - jungle, caves, buildings. Moving between them constantly during combat.

Problem: step from jungle into building and audio cuts instantly. Jungle ambience stops, building ambience starts. Jarring as hell.

Also enemy footsteps needed different ranges. Grenadier 75 meters away throwing explosives? Need to hear that for awareness. Debris falling 10 meters behind you? Should fade fast or it clutters the mix.

6.5s crossfades
10 curves
4 reverbs
11-155m range

Used Wwise states for zones. It tracks where you are and crossfades ambience over 6.5 seconds automatically. Walk into a building and jungle fades out while room tone fades in. No manual scripting.

Wrote 10 attenuation curves. Debris cuts at 11m, gates reach 155m, grenadier footsteps at 75m. Each sound has a distance that matches its importance.

Added 4 reverb channels controlled by game parameters. Walk into a cave and reverb increases automatically. Can tweak amounts in Wwise without recompiling.

First time writing attenuation curves from scratch. First try: too aggressive, enemies went silent at 30m. Second try: too conservative, everything audible at 100m+ and mix was mush. Took 4 playtests to dial it in. Lesson: iterate based on actual gameplay, not guessing.

Stomach Aches

Wwise 2024 4 Platforms 3 weeks

FPS platformer inside a human body. Three big zones: Brain, Mouth, Stomach. Needs to run on Windows, Mac, and Android phones with 2GB RAM.

Audio budget on Android: 15 MB max. Load all three zones at once and you're at 25+ MB. Game crashes on launch.

Windows handles uncompressed fine but Android needs aggressive compression. Different builds for different platforms playing the same content.

12 MB runtime
3.8 MB saved
80% smaller
4 platforms

Solution: dynamic soundbank loading. Only load audio for current zone. In Brain? Load Brain. Enter Mouth? Unload Brain, load Mouth. Saved 3.8 MB, total down to ~12 MB.

Used Wwise Silence plugin for ambience gaps. Instead of storing silent WAV files (which still eat memory), plugin generates procedural silence. Track with 5 seconds of silence went from 500 KB to 100 KB. 80% reduction.

Wrote C# scripts for zone triggers and distance mixing. Walk toward waterfall and 3 layers crossfade based on distance. Footsteps raycast down and auto-switch between gut, tongue, skull materials.

Only tested on Android emulator, not real hardware. Emulator says 12 MB but real phones might behave different - background processes, OS overhead, hardware quirks. Would need actual device profiling before calling this production-ready.
