How It Works

Low Latency Response

Optimized audio buffer sizes and ML inference pipeline to stay well below the 60ms threshold for rhythm games.

Learns Your Voice

Each person's beatbox sounds different. The system trains on your specific voice and microphone for maximum accuracy.

Smart Architecture

Decoupled Python analysis and Unity gameplay communicating via UDP/OSC ensures zero frame drops.

Technical Deep Dive

Spectral Analysis

Kick drums are deep, snares have noise. I trained the system to recognize these spectral fingerprints along with temporal envelopes.

Optimization Strategy

Smaller audio chunks (512 samples), faster ML inference (RandomForest), and UDP-based OSC communication.
