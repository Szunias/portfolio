Research

Measure technical claims in a running game.

Research is useful when it gives an audio team a production decision, including a clear reason not to use a technique.

Solo research · thesis published 2026

Can generative audio meet a real-time game latency target?

I built both ends of a UE5/Python prototype and recorded 600 timed generations. Procedural DSP reached 25 ms at p95; the fastest tested generator reached 1.9 seconds against a 150 ms interaction target. The practical result was to generate outside active play and cache the output.
